## Lessons Learned

1. **CEO requirements are absolute** - No patient page in Partners means none
2. **Evidence is mandatory** - Every claim needs peer-reviewed backing
3. **Consistency matters** - Same 3 CTAs everywhere for patients
4. **Naming is crucial** - 10-day not 14-day, SKIIN 3X not TriTest
5. **Structure follows strategy** - MVCP for GPs only, not standalone
