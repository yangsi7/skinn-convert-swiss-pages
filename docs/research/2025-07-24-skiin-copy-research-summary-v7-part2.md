**3\. Key External Evidence**

* **Silent AF and stroke risk:** Up to **70 %** of atrial fibrillation episodes are asymptomatic[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7252797/#:~:text=In%20patients%20with%20known%20AF%2C,yet%20paroxysmal%20silent%20AF%20has). AF contributes to about **20–30 %** of ischaemic strokes[heartandstroke.ca](https://www.heartandstroke.ca/heart-disease/conditions/atrial-fibrillation#:~:text=The%20risk%20of%20developing%20atrial,are%20stroke%20and%20heart%20failure). Early detection via continuous monitoring helps prevent strokes and guides timely treatment.

* **Extended monitoring superiority:** A 14‑day patch monitor detected arrhythmias in **66 %** of patients compared with **9 %** using a 24‑hour Holter monitor[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10264726/#:~:text=The%20detection%20rate%20with%2014%E2%80%90day,are%20between%207%20and%2010). Longer monitoring windows capture intermittent arrhythmias that would otherwise be missed.

* **Patient comfort:** Studies show that patch monitors are preferred by **93.7 %** of patients over traditional Holter devices[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4444741/#:~:text=The%20ZIO%20Patch%20and%20the,2014), emphasising the comfort revolution.

* **Carbon‑fibre electrodes durability:** Textile electrodes maintain functionality after at least **50 wash cycles**[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8029567/#:~:text=ABPM%20is%20the%20reference%20standard%2C,ABPM), ensuring long‑term usability.

* **ABPM as gold standard:** Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring measures BP every **15–30 min** during the day and **30–60 min** at night and is regarded as the **reference standard** for diagnosing hypertension[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8029567/#:~:text=Guidelines%20recommend%20the%20use%20of,ABPM%20are%20summarized%20in%20Table%C2%A02). ABPM can detect masked hypertension and nocturnal dipping[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8029567/#:~:text=ABPM%20is%20the%20reference%20standard%2C,ABPM).

* **Sleep analysis & arrhythmia:** Nocturnal arrhythmia prevalence increases dramatically in individuals with severe obstructive sleep apnoea—from **53 %** to **92 %**[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4262096/#:~:text=severity,atrial%20arrhythmias%2C%20ventricular%20arrhythmias%2C%20and)—highlighting the need for combined sleep and ECG monitoring.
