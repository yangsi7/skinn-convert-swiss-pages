   - Hospital visits are stressful
   - Bulky Holters are uncomfortable
   - One-time tests miss evolving conditions
   - Waiting rooms waste your time

3. **"You Need Screening If You Have:"**
   - Chest pain or palpitations
   - Family history of heart disease
   - Previous diagnosis requiring monitoring
   - No symptoms (prevention is key)

1. **"Early Detection Made Simple"**
   - Clinical-grade ECG from your sofa

   - No wires, no sticky patches

   - Blood pressure trends
   - Sleep breathing patterns
   - All in one comfortable garment

3. **"Prevention Becomes a Habit"**
   - Continuous monitoring, not snapshots
   - AI alerts before symptoms
   - Annual check-ups included
   - Your data, always accessible
