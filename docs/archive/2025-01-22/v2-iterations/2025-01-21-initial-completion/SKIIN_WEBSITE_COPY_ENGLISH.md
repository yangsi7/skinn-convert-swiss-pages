# SKIIN Switzerland Website Copy Guide - English

## Executive Summary

This document contains the complete English copy for the SKIIN Switzerland multilingual medical marketing website. It serves as the authoritative source for all text content, organized by page and component, with detailed guidelines for maintaining consistency across the entire site.

## Copy Style Guidelines & Themes

### Core Writing Principles

1. **Medical Credibility First**
   - Use clinical terminology accurately but accessibly
   - Always include certification mentions (CE certified, BAG recognition)
   - Reference Swiss medical standards and compliance
   - Support claims with statistics and evidence

2. **Swiss Cultural Sensitivity**
   - Emphasize Swiss quality, precision, and reliability
   - Respect for privacy and data protection
   - Professional tone with appropriate formality
   - Local references (Swiss cities, healthcare system, insurance models)

3. **Patient-Centric Language**
   - Empathetic and reassuring tone
   - Focus on comfort and convenience
   - Clear benefits over features
   - Address common concerns (privacy, discomfort, complexity)

4. **Conversion-Focused Structure**
   - Clear value propositions upfront
   - Multiple CTAs with urgency
   - Social proof (testimonials, statistics)
   - Trust signals (certifications, endorsements)

### Key Themes

1. **Comfort & Wearability**: "Finally, a heart monitor you'll actually want to wear"
2. **Swiss Excellence**: "Swiss precision meets cutting-edge technology"
3. **Early Detection**: "70% of heart rhythm disorders go unnoticed"
4. **Insurance Coverage**: "Fully covered by Swiss health insurance"
5. **Clinical Accuracy**: "98.6% accuracy rate, clinically validated"
6. **Simplicity**: "5 simple steps from prescription to results"

### Voice & Tone

- **Professional yet Approachable**: Medical authority without intimidation
- **Confident but Not Arrogant**: "Leading" not "the best"
- **Empowering**: Patients take control of their health
- **Reassuring**: Address anxieties about medical procedures
- **Action-Oriented**: Clear next steps and CTAs

### Visual Language Integration

Copy should complement visual elements:
- Short, scannable paragraphs for progressive scroll sections
- Bullet points for feature lists
- Statistics prominently displayed
- Testimonials with photos when possible
- Process steps with visual indicators

## How It Works Section

### Process Page (/how-it-works/process)

#### Page Goal
Clearly explain the 5-step process from prescription to results, addressing different insurance models.

#### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Prescription & Enrollment**
- Patient View: "You'll first answer a few questions to ensure SKIIN is right for you. SKIIN can coordinate with your doctor or a telemedicine physician to get an official prescription if needed."
- Physician View: "Identify patient & provide prescription. SKIIN can supply an order template for easy referral."
- Details: "Under Swiss law, a Holter test requires a physician's order. We make this easy, whether through your own GP or our partner doctors."

**Step 2: Device Delivery**
- Patient View: "The SKIIN kit is shipped directly to your home within 1-2 business days. The package includes the garment, sensor pod, charger, and simple instructions."
- Physician View: "No action needed - SKIIN handles device provision. Ensure patient has the mobile app installed."
- Details: "Setup is simple with no technician needed. Download the SKIIN app and follow the Quick Start Guide for device pairing."

**Step 3: Wearing & Monitoring**
- Patient View: "Wear the SKIIN band day and night for up to 14 days. Continue your normal activities - work, sleep, exercise. The device is water-resistant and comfortable."
- Physician View: "No action required during monitoring. Patient continues normal activities while data is collected continuously."
- Details: "The sensor records every heartbeat silently. If you feel symptoms, mark them in the app to help correlate events in the analysis."

**Step 4: Data Analysis**
- Patient View: "Your ECG data is analyzed using advanced AI algorithms, then reviewed by certified cardiologists. All findings are verified by medical professionals."
- Physician View: "SKIIN's team analyzes data with AI + human review. You may be contacted for any urgent findings if preferred."
- Details: "We use AI to screen for arrhythmias, but every SKIIN recording gets human expert review before finalizing for accuracy."

**Step 5: Report & Follow-Up**
- Patient View: "Your doctor receives a comprehensive report and will discuss the results with you. If urgent issues are found, we expedite notification."
- Physician View: "Receive detailed report via secure portal. Review findings and follow up with patient. Full disclosure ECG available if needed."
- Details: "Reports include overview of findings, any detected arrhythmias with timestamps, heart rate trends, and clinical interpretations."

## Medical Terminology Guidelines

### Approved Terms
- Cardiac monitoring (not heart tracking)
- Arrhythmia detection (not irregular heartbeat finding)
- Clinical-grade ECG (not medical-quality heart monitoring)
- Continuous monitoring (not always-on tracking)
- Textile sensors (not fabric technology)

### Swiss-Specific Terms
- Hausarztmodell (GP model)
- Telmed (telemedicine model)
- HMO (health maintenance organization)
- Grundversicherung (basic insurance)
- BAG (Bundesamt für Gesundheit/Federal Office of Public Health)

## Appendix: Swiss Market Considerations

### Cultural Nuances
- Swiss German speakers prefer direct, factual communication
- French-speaking Swiss appreciate more elaborate, polite language
- Italian-speaking Swiss (Ticino) value warmth and personal connection
- Always use formal address (Sie/Vous) in initial communications

### Regulatory Language
- Always mention Swissmedic compliance
- Reference BAG (Federal Office of Public Health) recognition
- Include CE marking information
- Specify data storage in Switzerland

### Insurance Specifics
- Grundversicherung (basic insurance) coverage is key selling point
- Zusatzversicherung (supplementary insurance) for preventive screening
- Franchise considerations in messaging
- Canton-specific insurance variations
