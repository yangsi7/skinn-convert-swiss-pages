# SKIIN Switzerland Website Copy Guide - English

## Executive Summary

This document contains the complete English copy for the SKIIN Switzerland multilingual medical marketing website. It serves as the authoritative source for all text content, organized by page and component, with detailed guidelines for maintaining consistency across the entire site.

## Copy Style Guidelines & Themes

### Core Writing Principles

1. **Medical Credibility First**
   - Use clinical terminology accurately but accessibly
   - Always include certification mentions (CE certified, BAG recognition)
   - Reference Swiss medical standards and compliance
   - Support claims with statistics and evidence

2. **Swiss Cultural Sensitivity**
   - Emphasize Swiss quality, precision, and reliability
   - Respect for privacy and data protection
   - Professional tone with appropriate formality
   - Local references (Swiss cities, healthcare system, insurance models)

3. **Patient-Centric Language**
   - Empathetic and reassuring tone
   - Focus on comfort and convenience
   - Clear benefits over features
   - Address common concerns (privacy, discomfort, complexity)

4. **Conversion-Focused Structure**
   - Clear value propositions upfront
   - Multiple CTAs with urgency
   - Social proof (testimonials, statistics)
   - Trust signals (certifications, endorsements)

### Key Themes

1. **Comfort & Wearability**: "Finally, a heart monitor you'll actually want to wear"
2. **Swiss Excellence**: "Swiss precision meets cutting-edge technology"
3. **Early Detection**: "70% of heart rhythm disorders go unnoticed"
4. **Insurance Coverage**: "Fully covered by Swiss health insurance"
5. **Clinical Accuracy**: "98.6% accuracy rate, clinically validated"
6. **Simplicity**: "5 simple steps from prescription to results"

### Voice & Tone

- **Professional yet Approachable**: Medical authority without intimidation
- **Confident but Not Arrogant**: "Leading" not "the best"
- **Empowering**: Patients take control of their health
- **Reassuring**: Address anxieties about medical procedures
- **Action-Oriented**: Clear next steps and CTAs

### Visual Language Integration

Copy should complement visual elements:
- Short, scannable paragraphs for progressive scroll sections
- Bullet points for feature lists
- Statistics prominently displayed
- Testimonials with photos when possible
- Process steps with visual indicators

- Telmed (telemedicine model)
- HMO (health maintenance organization)

## Appendix: Swiss Market Considerations

### Cultural Nuances
- Swiss German speakers prefer direct, factual communication
- French-speaking Swiss appreciate more elaborate, polite language
- Italian-speaking Swiss (Ticino) value warmth and personal connection
- Always use formal address (Sie/Vous) in initial communications

### Regulatory Language
- Always mention Swissmedic compliance
- Reference BAG (Federal Office of Public Health) recognition
- Include CE marking information
- Specify data storage in Switzerland

### Insurance Specifics
- Grundversicherung (basic insurance) coverage is key selling point
- Zusatzversicherung (supplementary insurance) for preventive screening
- Franchise considerations in messaging
- Canton-specific insurance variations
