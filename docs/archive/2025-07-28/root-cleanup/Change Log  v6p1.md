## Rationale Summary

Version 6.0 responds directly to the CEO’s feedback by strengthening preventive urgency, integrating ABPM and sleep analytics, clarifying the AI process, emphasising the comfort and removability of the garment, updating the mission & heritage narrative, unifying professional pages and refining CTAs. All statistical claims have been grounded in peer‑reviewed evidence, and regulatory and compliance language has been updated to reflect MDR Class IIa and Swissmedic requirements. The partners page now serves as a comprehensive hub for patients and professionals, and the overall copy is more patient‑centric, empathetic and conversion‑oriented.
