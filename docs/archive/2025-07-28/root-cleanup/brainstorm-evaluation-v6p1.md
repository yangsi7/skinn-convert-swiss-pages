# Brainstorming & Evaluation of Proposed Copy Updates

This document captures potential solutions for each CEO comment and evaluates them against the style guidelines, tone of voice, page objectives and broader website goals. Each entry includes a short description of the idea, an estimated importance score (1–10, where 10 is most aligned with objectives), and a rationale. High‑scoring options are recommended for implementation.

## 1\. Emphasise preventive urgency & longevity

| Idea | Score | Rationale |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Integrate “Screen early. Live longer.” into the hero headline** | **9** | Captures the dual themes of urgency and longevity succinctly; aligns with the marketing plan’s focus on early detection. |
| **Use “Your heart. Your family. Your control.” as a variant** | 7 | Emphasises control and family but may dilute urgency. Good for A/B testing. |
| **Replace “revolution” with “leading innovation” or remove it entirely** | 10 | Avoids hyperbolic marketing language while maintaining credibility; meets CEO instruction. |
| **Highlight longevity in the subheadline (“invest in decades, not days”)** | 8 | Reinforces the longevity & quality of life pillar; supports preventive urgency but may lengthen the subheadline. |

**Preferred solution:** Use “Screen early. Live longer. From home.” as the primary headline and test “Your heart. Your family. Your control.” as a secondary option. Remove words like “revolution.”

## Summary

The brainstorming process prioritises solutions that align strongly with the strategic directives, evidence base and user requirements. High‑scoring options will form the foundation of the revised copy specification. Lower‑scoring or redundant ideas (e.g., reusing marketing slogans verbatim, adding multiple similar call‑outs) will be omitted or used sparingly.
