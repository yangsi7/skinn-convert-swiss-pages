## Copy Style Guidelines & Themes

### Core Writing Principles

1. **Patient‑centric language comes first.** Write with empathy and reassurance. Address both patients and caregivers directly (using “you,” “your family”) and focus on benefits—comfort, convenience, peace of mind—rather than features.

2. **Use plain, medically accurate language.** Explain clinical concepts without jargon. Introduce terms like “irregular heart rhythm” before “arrhythmia,” and avoid sensational or unsupported claims.

3. **Swiss medical excellence and regulatory trust.** Emphasise that SKIIN is MDR Class IIa certified and registered with Swissmedic, complies with GDPR and the Swiss Data Protection Act and stores data exclusively in Switzerland. Highlight Myant’s expertise in textile sensing and the integration of MediCalgorithmics’ AI.

4. **Conversion‑focused structure.** Present clear value propositions early, support them with evidence and emotional storytelling, and place conversion CTAs in strategic positions (hero, after problem/solution, before testimonials, footer and as a floating button on mobile). Professional audiences receive dedicated CTAs to book a demo or join the GP network.

5. **Cultural sensitivity follows patient‑centricity.** Localise the message for German, French and Italian speakers using formal address (Sie/Vous/Lei) and region‑appropriate tone, but keep empathy and clarity paramount.

### Brand Voice Evolution

* **Current state:** Professional, clinical and Swiss‑quality focused.

* **Target state:** Empathetic expert—maintain clinical authority while adding warmth, urgency and accessibility. Put preventive health urgency first, followed by emotional storytelling, comfort revolution, longevity & quality of life, arrhythmia–stroke connection, Swiss medical excellence, caregiver & family focus and accessibility assurance.

### Messaging Pillars (Priority Order)

1. **Preventive health urgency:** Up to 70 % of AF episodes are silent; early detection prevents strokes. Encourage visitors to act now.

2. **Emotional storytelling:** Link heart health to cherished life moments—birthdays, hikes and family milestones. Use anonymised patient stories to illustrate benefits.

3. **Comfort revolution:** Highlight soft, wire‑free textile electrodes that are water‑resistant, washable and removable. “Forget you’re being monitored.”

4. **Longevity & quality of life:** Frame screening as an investment in decades, not days. Emphasise that monitoring helps individuals enjoy more years with loved ones.

5. **Arrhythmia–stroke connection:** Explain that AF contributes to ≈20 % of ischaemic strokes and continuous monitoring can reduce risk.

6. **Swiss medical excellence:** Highlight MDR Class IIa certification, Swissmedic registration, local data residency and integration with Swiss healthcare models (Hausarztmodell, HMO, Telmed).

7. **Caregiver & family focus:** Speak to loved ones (e.g., daughters ordering for fathers) and emphasise their role in preventive care.

8. **Accessibility assurance:** Stress that screening is fully covered by basic insurance with a prescription, available in multiple languages and supported by personal assistance.

### Voice & Tone

* **Professional yet approachable:** Maintain authority without intimidation. Be direct, clear and warm.

* **Confident but not arrogant:** Use language such as “leading innovation” or “clinically proven” rather than “revolution.”

* **Empowering:** Encourage users to take control of their heart health and engage their doctors.

* **Reassuring:** Acknowledge anxieties about medical procedures and address them (e.g., comfort, privacy, ease of use).

* **Action‑oriented:** Use clear CTAs and next steps throughout the copy.

### Visual Language Integration

* Use short, scannable paragraphs and bullet points. Support claims with icons or simple illustrations (e.g., the AI analysis flow).

* Display statistics prominently with concise text and icons.

* Use real patient photos and doctor portraits where possible; avoid cliché stock images.

* Show process steps with icons to guide the reader.

* *When will I get my results?* Your doctor receives the report within 24–48 hours after monitoring completion. Urgent findings are flagged immediately.

* *How is the data analysed?* ECG and other vital‑sign data are processed by the DeepRhythm Platform and reviewed by qualified technicians; a cardiologist signs off before the report is sent.

## Appendix: Swiss Market Considerations

* **Cultural nuances:** Swiss German speakers prefer direct, factual communication; French‑speaking Swiss appreciate more elaborate and polite language; Italian‑speaking Swiss value warmth and personal connection. Use formal address (Sie/Vous/Lei) in initial communications.

* **Language support:** German (DE), French (FR), Italian (IT) and English (EN).

* **Regulatory language:** Always mention Swissmedic compliance and MDR Class IIa certification; specify data storage in Switzerland; emphasise GDPR and Swiss DPA compliance.

* **Insurance specifics:** Grundversicherung coverage is a key selling point; highlight supplementary insurance for preventive screening; consider franchise and canton variations.
