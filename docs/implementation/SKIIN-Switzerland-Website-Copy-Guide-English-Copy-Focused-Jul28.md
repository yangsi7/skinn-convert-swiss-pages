## **Copy Style Guidelines & Themes**

### **Core Writing Principles**

1. **Patient‑centric language first.** Use “you” and “your family” to address patients and caregivers directly. Focus on benefits—comfort, convenience, peace of mind—rather than technical specifications.

2. **Plain, accurate language.** Explain medical concepts without jargon. For example, introduce “irregular heart rhythm” before using “arrhythmia.”

3. **Swiss medical excellence & trust.** Emphasise MDR Class IIa certification, Swissmedic registration, ISO 13485 quality management and ISO/IEC 27001 cloud security. Highlight that data are stored exclusively in Switzerland.

4. **Conversion‑focused structure.** Present clear value propositions early, support them with evidence and emotional storytelling, and place CTAs strategically (hero, after problem/solution, before testimonials, footer). Professional CTAs are only on partner pages.

5. **Cultural sensitivity.** In translations, use formal address (Sie/Vous/Lei). Acknowledge Switzerland’s multilingual nature and note the availability of support in German, French and Italian.

### **Brand Voice Evolution**

* **From clinical to empathetic expert.** Maintain clinical authority while injecting warmth and urgency. Avoid hype; use “innovation” or “leading” instead of “revolution.”  
* **Empowering & reassuring.** Encourage visitors to take control of their heart health. Address anxieties about monitoring by emphasising comfort, privacy and easy setup.  
* **Action‑oriented.** Use imperative verbs (“Start,” “Check,” “Join,” “Book”) and emphasise that early screening adds years to life.

### **Messaging Pillars (Priority Order)**

1. **Preventive health urgency & longevity.** Up to 70 % of arrhythmias show no symptoms[pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7252797/#:~:text=In%20patients%20with%20known%20AF%2C,yet%20paroxysmal%20silent%20AF%20has); early detection prevents strokes[heartandstroke.ca](https://www.heartandstroke.ca/heart-disease/conditions/atrial-fibrillation#:~:text=The%20risk%20of%20developing%20atrial,are%20stroke%20and%20heart%20failure). Screening today means celebrating more birthdays tomorrow.

2. **Emotional storytelling.** Connect heart health to personal milestones—birthdays, mountain hikes, grandchildren’s graduations. Share anonymised patient stories illustrating comfort and peace of mind.

3. **Comfort revolution.** Describe soft, wire‑free textile electrodes that are water‑resistant, washable and removable. Patients can take off the garment to shower and carry on with life as usual.

4. **Longevity & quality of life.** Frame screening as an investment in decades. Good heart health allows people to live younger, longer.

5. **Arrhythmia–stroke connection & Silent Triad.** Explain that AF contributes to 20–30 % of strokes[heartandstroke.ca](https://www.heartandstroke.ca/heart-disease/conditions/atrial-fibrillation#:~:text=The%20risk%20of%20developing%20atrial,are%20stroke%20and%20heart%20failure). Present the Silent Triad (arrhythmia, hypertension and sleep apnoea) as a hidden threat that SKIIN addresses through tri‑modal monitoring.

6. **Swiss medical excellence.** Stress local certification, data residency and integration with Swiss healthcare models (Hausarztmodell/GP model, HMO, Telmed). Mention Myant’s collaboration with Swiss cardiologists and insurers.

7. **Caregiver & family focus.** Recognise that loved ones often initiate screening for older relatives. Speak to adult children, spouses and employers who want to protect someone they care about.

8. **Accessibility & reimbursement.** Emphasise that SKIIN is fully covered by basic insurance when prescribed, with transparent self‑pay options. Support is available in multiple languages and through local helplines.

### **Visual Language Integration**

* Break copy into short, scannable paragraphs. Use bullet lists (with icons in design) to summarise features and benefits.

* Display statistics prominently in cards or call‑outs.

* Pair testimonials with photos (once available); use placeholders until final images are ready.

* Use simple illustrations to depict the AI analysis flow and tri‑modal monitoring system.
