# SKIIN Switzerland Website Copy Guide - English
Version 2.0 - January 22, 2025

## Executive Summary

This document contains the complete English copy for the SKIIN Switzerland multilingual medical marketing website. It serves as the authoritative source for all text content, organized by page and component, with detailed guidelines for maintaining consistency across the entire site. This version implements comprehensive updates based on stakeholder feedback, emphasizing emotional messaging, clearer CTAs, and enhanced conversion optimization.

## Copy Style Guidelines & Themes

### Core Writing Principles

1. **Medical Credibility First**
   - Use clinical terminology accurately but accessibly
   - Always include certification mentions (CE certified, BAG recognized)
   - Reference Swiss medical standards and compliance
   - Support claims with statistics and evidence

2. **Swiss Cultural Sensitivity**
   - Emphasize Swiss quality, precision, and reliability
   - Respect for privacy and data protection
   - Professional tone with appropriate formality
   - Local references (Swiss cities, healthcare system, insurance models)

3. **Patient-Centric Language**
   - Empathetic and reassuring tone
   - Focus on comfort and convenience
   - Clear benefits over features
   - Address common concerns (privacy, discomfort, complexity)

4. **Conversion-Focused Structure**
   - Clear value propositions upfront
   - Multiple CTAs with urgency
   - Social proof (testimonials, statistics)
   - Trust signals (certifications, endorsements)

### Brand Voice Evolution
- **Current State**: Professional, clinical, Swiss-quality focused
- **Target State**: Empathetic expert - maintaining authority while adding warmth and urgency

### Enhanced Messaging Pillars

1. **Swiss Medical Excellence** (Maintain)
   - "Swiss precision meets compassionate care"
   - CE certified, BAG recognized
   - Local medical board endorsements

2. **Comfort Revolution** (Enhance)
   - "Finally, a heart monitor you'll forget you're wearing"
   - No wires, no patches, just peace of mind
   - Machine washable, life-compatible

3. **Preventive Urgency** (New)
   - "70% of heart issues are silent - until they're not"
   - "Early detection isn't optional, it's essential"
   - Link to stroke prevention clearly

4. **Longevity Promise** (New)
   - "Add years to your life, life to your years"
   - "Invest in decades, not just days"
   - Future-focused messaging

5. **Family Impact** (New)
   - "Every heartbeat echoes in someone's heart"
   - "For you, for them, for tomorrow"
   - Caregiver-inclusive language

6. **Accessibility Assurance** (Enhance)
   - "Fully covered, fully supported, fully Swiss"
   - Multiple pathways to care
   - Language and location inclusive

### Voice & Tone

- **Professional yet Approachable**: Medical authority without intimidation
- **Confident but Not Arrogant**: "Leading" not "the best"
- **Empowering**: Patients take control of their health
- **Reassuring**: Address anxieties about medical procedures
- **Action-Oriented**: Clear next steps and CTAs

### Visual Language Integration

Copy should complement visual elements:
- Short, scannable paragraphs for progressive scroll sections
- Bullet points for feature lists
- Statistics prominently displayed
- Testimonials with photos when possible
- Process steps with visual indicators

- Always use formal address (Sie/Vous/Lei) in initial communications

- Always mention Swissmedic compliance
- Reference BAG (Federal Office of Public Health) recognition
- Include CE marking information
- Specify data storage in Switzerland
