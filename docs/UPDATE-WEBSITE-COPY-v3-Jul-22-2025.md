
# SKIIN Switzerland Website Copy Update Specification (Revised)

**Version 3.0 – July 22, 2025**
*(Supersedes Version 2.0 dated Jan 22, 2025)*

## Executive Summary

This updated specification outlines comprehensive copy revisions for the SKIIN Switzerland website. Changes incorporate stakeholder feedback and deep research findings to refine our messaging. The goals are to **strengthen emotional appeal**, **emphasize preventive health and longevity**, and **tailor value propositions** for multiple audiences (patients, doctors, partners) while maintaining Swiss cultural sensitivity and clinical accuracy. All content aligns with SKIIN’s established visual design and brand voice. **Statistics and medical claims are validated by research** (see RESEARCH.md), and the tone is empathetic, empowering, and trustworthy – avoiding fear tactics and ensuring clarity in all four supported languages (German, French, Italian, English).

## 1. Global Changes

### 1.1 Product Naming Updates

* **Holter Monitoring** → **10‑Day Heart Screening**
  *(We refer to the service as a “10-Day Heart Screening” instead of “Holter” to emphasize duration and purpose in user-friendly terms.)*
* **TriTest** → **SKIIN 3X Screening™**
  *(This product name highlights the triple-monitoring aspect: it monitors heart rhythm, blood-pressure trends, and sleep breathing in one.)*
* **Self-Pay Plan Labels:** Use descriptive names: **3‑Day Screening**, **5‑Day Screening**, and **10‑Day Screening (Gold Standard)** for the direct-pay packages. Pricing details for each are listed in the Pricing section. Avoid referring to “Holter” duration in days publicly; use these names consistently across the site and materials.

### 1.2 Core Themes (Voice & Messaging Pillars)

Maintain the existing core themes: **Swiss precision & quality**, **medical credibility**, **insurance coverage**, **comfort & wearability**, and **clinical accuracy**. **Integrate the following new messaging pillars across all copy:**

* **Longevity & Quality of Life:** Inspire users with the promise of a healthier, longer life. E.g., use the line *“Add years to your life, and life to your years”* to encourage proactive heart screening. Content should frame SKIIN as a means to **enjoy more moments** – from major milestones to everyday joys – by catching issues early.

* **Preventive Health Urgency:** Emphasize that many serious heart rhythm issues show no outward symptoms until it’s too late. For example: *“Don’t wait for symptoms – up to 70% of atrial fibrillation episodes may be completely symptom-free”*. This underlines the importance of early detection. The tone remains encouraging: early action is empowering, not alarmist.

* **Arrhythmia–Stroke Connection:** Clearly communicate the link between undetected arrhythmias (like AFib) and stroke risk. State that *atrial fibrillation is a factor in 20–30% of ischemic strokes* – meaning roughly 1 in 3 strokes can be tied to heart rhythm disorders. Follow this with a hopeful message that **screening and early intervention help prevent such outcomes** (e.g., by enabling timely treatment). This theme adds gravity to our offering, but must be used alongside solutions to avoid fear.

* **Emotional Storytelling (Human Impact):** Weave in human stories and imagery. Every detection is more than a clinical event – it’s *“another birthday celebrated, another mountain climbed, another life uninterrupted”*. Our copy will include brief vignettes or examples that illustrate how SKIIN has impacted individuals and families (even if anonymized), to create an emotional connection. The tone is uplifting and personal, putting the reader in the shoes of someone whose life was positively changed by early detection.

* **Caregiver & Family Messaging:** Reflect the perspective of loved ones who encourage preventative care. Lines like *“For yourself, and for those who love you”* or *“Your family needs you healthy”* should appear where appropriate (e.g., in hero or CTA sections, or in caregiver-targeted content). This theme underscores that using SKIIN is an act of care not just for oneself but for one’s family. It adds an altruistic motivation for the user and broadens the emotional appeal.

These integrated themes ensure the copy simultaneously appeals to logic (statistics, facts) and emotion (stories, aspirations), in line with our research-driven recommendations.

### 1.3 Language Support & Localization

SKIIN’s website is fully multilingual. We now **add Italian** as the fourth language across the site (in addition to German, French, and English). Update all language selector components and any text references to languages accordingly (e.g., “available in German, French, Italian, and English”).

Localization guidelines to implement globally:

* Use formal address in each language (German “Sie”, French “vous”, Italian “Lei”) consistently in all copy. The tone in translations should remain respectful, clear, and slightly more formal than English, reflecting Swiss cultural norms.
* Ensure region-specific terminology: e.g., use Swiss French terms for numbers (septante, huitante, nonante) and Swiss health system terms (use “assurance de base” in French for basic insurance, “obbligatoria” in Italian if needed, etc.), and avoid German “ß” (use “ss”) in Swiss German text.
* All stats and claims must be translated exactly (no rounding or altering numbers) to remain consistent across languages. Provide translators with context or references for each claim so they maintain accuracy.
* Maintain the same structure and messaging hierarchy in each language’s version. Each section, headline, bullet, and CTA in this spec should appear in the other language sites with equivalent impact, not omitted or significantly rearranged. Only adapt phrasing to fit linguistic and cultural nuance.

Before deployment, perform a thorough cross-language review to ensure **tone, terminology, and claims are aligned** in German, French, Italian, and English copies.

Our app (or the device’s built-in memory) securely transmits data in the background. You will hardly notice it, aside from perhaps checking the app occasionally for confirmation. Live normally, and remember: **don’t worry if you remove it briefly for hygiene or changing clothes; just wear it as much as possible for the prescribed days** for best results.
5. **Complete & Return:** Once your monitoring period (3, 5, or 10 days) is over, or you’ve collected enough data, you’ll get a notification. Simply take off the garment, put it back in the provided box, and use the **prepaid return envelope** to mail it back to us. It’s hassle-free – drop it in any SwissPost mailbox.
6. **Data Analysis:** As your data comes in (and after we receive the device), our system and medical team get to work. AI algorithms flag any irregular patterns in your heart data, and then our **cardiology experts review everything** to verify and compile your results. This hybrid analysis ensures accuracy and that no important finding is missed.
7. **Results & Follow-Up:** Within 48 hours of receiving your returned kit, your results are ready. You (via the app or secure email) and your doctor get a comprehensive report. It will tell you if any arrhythmias or concerns were detected, and provide recommendations (e.g., “consult a cardiologist” or “all clear for now, recheck in a year”). If something urgent is found, rest assured we will contact you promptly. You can always reach out with questions, and we encourage you to discuss the report with your physician for any needed next steps.

This evidence supports our “patients actually complete the test” messaging (though we avoid quoting “95%” unless we have an exact stat confirmed).
* **Textile Sensor Efficacy:** Studies on graphene-coated textile electrodes show they are **comparable in signal quality to traditional gel electrodes**, even after repeated washing. This backs our claim of “machine washable without signal loss” and that comfort doesn’t compromise accuracy.
