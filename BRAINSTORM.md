# Brainstorming - Component Consolidation Approaches

## Approach Options

### Option 1: "SKIIN Advantage" Mega-Component
**Score: 9/10**
- Combines technology, comfort, and quality into one compelling section
- Uses large visuals with layered information
- Interactive elements reveal details without clutter
- **Pros**: Maximum impact, modern design, reduces redundancy
- **Cons**: Requires careful information architecture

### Option 2: Separate Tech/Comfort Components
**Score: 6/10**
- Keep technology and comfort as distinct sections
- Each with unique visual treatment
- **Pros**: Clear separation of concepts
- **Cons**: Still somewhat redundant, less innovative

### Option 3: Journey-Based Layout
**Score: 7/10**
- Organize by user journey stages
- Progressive disclosure of benefits
- **Pros**: Logical flow, story-driven
- **Cons**: May feel too linear

### Option 4: Visual-First Gallery
**Score: 8/10**
- Large images with minimal text overlays
- Click/hover for detailed information
- **Pros**: Highly visual, modern aesthetic
- **Cons**: Requires strong visual assets

## Component Design Patterns

### For "SKIIN Advantage" Component:
1. **Split Layout** (Score: 9/10)
   - Left: Product showcase with multiple angles
   - Right: Key benefits with expandable details

2. **Tabbed Interface** (Score: 7/10)
   - Tabs for Technology, Comfort, Quality
   - Visual changes with each tab

3. **Scroll-Triggered Reveals** (Score: 8/10)
   - Benefits appear as user scrolls
   - Parallax effects on images

### For "Home Holter Study" Component:
1. **Process Timeline** (Score: 9/10)
   - Visual steps with clinical images
   - Emphasis on convenience and results

2. **Before/After Comparison** (Score: 7/10)
   - Traditional vs SKIIN approach
   - Side-by-side benefits

3. **Interactive Demo** (Score: 8/10)
   - Walk through the experience
   - Show real app/report screenshots
