### Basic Coverage Overview

"SKIIN's 14-day ECG monitoring is recognized as medically necessary diagnostics by Swiss health insurers. When prescribed by a physician, it's covered under basic insurance (LaMal/KVG) using the same codes as traditional Holter monitoring."

**What This Means:**
- Counts toward your annual deductible
- 90% covered after deductible is met
- 10% copay up to CHF 700 annual maximum
- No prior authorization required

### Coverage by Insurance Model

**Standard Model (Free Choice)**
- See any doctor for prescription
- Direct billing to insurance
- Standard deductible/copay rules apply
- Coverage: 90% after deductible

**GP Model (Hausarztmodell)**
- First visit your designated GP
- GP provides prescription or referral
- Same coverage as standard model
- Some insurers waive copay

**HMO Model**
- Visit HMO clinic physician
- Internal prescription process
- Often lower copays
- Coverage: 90-100% after deductible

**Telmed Model**
- Call insurance medical hotline first
- Receive authorization number
- Then see approved physician
- Coverage varies by insurer

- SKIIN service cost: CHF 450
