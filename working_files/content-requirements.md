## Content Guidelines

### Medical Accuracy
- All health claims must be evidence-based
- Include appropriate disclaimers
- Reference Swiss medical standards
- Use precise medical terminology with patient-friendly explanations

### Tone & Voice
**For Patients**
- Warm, reassuring, empowering
- Clear explanations without condescension
- Focus on comfort and peace of mind
- Address common fears and concerns

**For Physicians**
- Professional, evidence-based
- Efficient and practical
- Respect for clinical expertise
- Focus on diagnostic value

### Swiss Cultural Considerations
- Formal address (Sie/vous) in all languages
- Emphasis on precision and quality
- Privacy and data protection prominence
- Canton-specific information where relevant

### SEO Requirements
**Primary Keywords**
